Gimbal Cannon Design

Team Project SOLIDWORKS + Autodesk Inventor

Overview

A gimbal cannon to be used for the upcoming RoboMaster tournament. My role was to redesign the gimbal cannon itself, which is essentially a mechanism that fires projectiles the size of ping-pong balls. Accuracy and range are key here!

Highlights

  • Designed, prototyped, and tested different components and experimented with multiple configurations.
  • Robot was brought up to 90% firing accuracy within a 20cm radius, and a 50% faster firing speed compared to previous years' model.
  • Worked with 3D-printed parts and gained exposure to SOLIDWORKS and part modelling.

How It Works

1. Loading

Small projectile ball comes up the tubing shaft and prepares for launch.

2. Release

After unlock, ball is released into spinning wheels to be fired.

3. Fire

Ball reaches target up to specified range.
