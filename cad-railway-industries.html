Company Overview

CAD Railway Industries is a Canadian company that specializes in the design, manufacturing, and maintenance of railway components and systems. The company provides a wide range of products and services to the railway industry and companies within it, including track components, signaling systems, and maintenance services; one of which being Metrolinx, a regional public transportation agency in the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area. Working in the Toronto office, my role was primarily focused on supporting the engineering team in their documentation and design efforts towards the maintenance of all of Metrolinx's railway trains and components. This included creating technical drawings, managing maintenance procedures, and developing an engineering manual for the department.
